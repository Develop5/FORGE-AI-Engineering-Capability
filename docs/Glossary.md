
# Glossary

## Business Requirement

A statement of a business need, capability, outcome, or expected behavior that the system must satisfy.

A Business Requirement may be explicitly documented or identified from evidence where it is expressed indirectly, including User Stories, Acceptance Criteria, Bug Expected Results, Technical Requirements, Epics, Test Cases, or other relevant Evidence Sources.

---

## Functional Specification

A precise, verifiable, and sufficiently detailed description of the behavior or conditions that the system must meet in order to satisfy a Business Requirement.

A Functional Specification may be synthesized by FORGE when it is required to structure or clarify the relationship between Business Requirements and Test Cases.

A Functional Specification does not by itself demonstrate that a Business Requirement is covered.

---

## Use Case

A description of how a user or another actor interacts with a system to achieve a particular goal.

---

## Test Case

A defined set of conditions, inputs, steps, and expected results used to verify a specific behavior or outcome of a system.

---

## Evidence Source

A source of project information that FORGE can analyze to identify, clarify, or trace Business Requirements and Test Cases.

Examples include:

* Confluence pages
* Jira Test Cases
* Jira Bugs
* Jira User Stories
* Jira Epics
* Technical Requirements
* Acceptance Criteria
* Other relevant project documentation

---

## Requirements Discovery

The FORGE capability that analyzes available Evidence Sources to identify and consolidate Business Requirements and detect requirement issues.

Requirements Discovery may identify:

* Business Requirements
* Conflicts
* Unresolved Dependencies
* Ambiguities
* Clarification Questions

---

## Clarification

The process through which FORGE presents questions resulting from ambiguities, conflicts, unresolved dependencies, or insufficient evidence, and incorporates the answers provided by the user.

Unresolved questions may remain visible as risks when the user decides not to resolve them.

---

## Traceability

The explicit relationship between Business Requirements and Test Cases, supported by the available evidence.

Traceability allows FORGE to determine which Test Cases provide evidence for each Business Requirement.

A Business Requirement may be related to multiple Test Cases, and a Test Case may be related to multiple Business Requirements.

---

## Coverage

The percentage of Business Requirements that are covered by Test Cases.

Formula:

```text
(# of Business Requirements covered by tests) / (Total Business Requirements)
````

A Business Requirement is considered covered when it has at least one related Test Case.

Coverage is expressed as a percentage.

---

## Current Coverage

Coverage calculated from the existing Test Cases available to FORGE.

Current Coverage represents the coverage supported by the Test Cases that already exist in the available evidence.

---

## Projected Coverage

Coverage that would result from adding the Test Cases generated by FORGE to improve Business Requirement Coverage.

Projected Coverage is not evidence of executed Test Coverage because generated Test Cases have not necessarily been executed or validated.

---

## Coverage Report

The result provided by FORGE containing the Business Requirement Coverage analysis and the information necessary to understand the current coverage and remaining functional risk.

The Coverage Report may include:

* Business Requirements
* Related Test Cases
* Current Coverage
* Business Requirements without identified coverage
* Insufficient evidence
* Functional inconsistencies
* Remaining functional risk

---

## Functional Risk

Risk related to the functional coverage evidenced by the available Business Requirements and Test Cases.

Functional Risk may result from:

* Business Requirements without identified Test Case coverage
* Insufficient evidence
* Unresolved requirement issues
* Functional inconsistencies
* Unresolved clarification questions

---

## Requirement Issues

Problems identified during Requirements Discovery or Clarification that may affect the understanding or validation of Business Requirements.

Requirement Issues include:

* Ambiguities
* Conflicts among requirements
* Contradictions among requirements or within a requirement
* Circular dependencies
