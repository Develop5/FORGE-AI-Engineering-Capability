# Decisions

| ID                                                                                      | Date       | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | Reason                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| D-001                                                                                   | 2026-08-15 | FORGE does not define a Primary User or a preferred professional role.                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | FORGE is intended to be usable by users in different roles involved in requirements, testing, product, engineering, or related activities.                                                                                                                          |
| D-002                                                                                   | 2026-08-07 | The primary domain outcome of the MVP is the Coverage Result produced by Coverage Analysis.                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Coverage Result represents the highest-value domain outcome of the MVP. Its presentation or reporting is a separate concern from the domain result itself.                                                                                                          |
| D-003                                                                                   | 2026-08-07 | Requirements Discovery is an internal capability, not the product itself.                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | FORGE is a product composed of multiple capabilities, with Business Requirement Coverage and its improvement being core product outcomes.                                                                                                                           |
| D-004                                                                                   | 2026-08-08 | FORGE is designed to analyse heterogeneous and incomplete Evidence Sources, including Jira Test Cases, Business Requirement documentation, User Stories, Bugs, Use Cases, Technical documentation, and other relevant evidence. The MVP scope is intentionally narrower and is defined separately. | The capability must support different combinations of available evidence because Business Requirements and testing information may be dispersed, incomplete, or inconsistently documented.                                                                          |
| D-005                                                                                   | 2026-08-07 | FORGE supports collaboration between roles but does not modify existing organizational responsibilities.                                                                                                                                                                                           | Collaboration among different roles provides high value and a smooth process.                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| D-006                                                                                   | 2026-08-07 | Glossary definitions are added progressively when discussions or identified ambiguities require them.                                                                                                                                                                                              | Avoid defining universally understood terms prematurely and keep the glossary focused on terms that require explicit clarification.                                                                                                                                 |
| D-007                                                                                   | 2026-08-15 | Specifications are intermediate analysis artefacts. A Specification does not become a Business Requirement and does not participate directly in Business Requirement Coverage. Approved Specifications may be used as contextual information for Test Case Generation.                             | Specifications may provide useful context without altering the Business Requirement set or the Coverage calculation.                                                                                                                                                |
| D-009                                                                                   | 2026-08-13 | The MVP Evidence Sources are expanded to include Confluence pages, Jira Test Cases, Jira Bugs, Jira User Stories, and Jira Epics. Business Requirements may be identified from explicit or implicit evidence across these sources.                                                                 | Business Requirements may be distributed across project artefacts and may appear in Business Requirements documentation, Technical Requirements, Acceptance Criteria, User Story descriptions, Bug Expected Results, Epics, Test Cases, or other relevant evidence. |
| The MVP must demonstrate this heterogeneous evidence analysis.                          |            |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| D-010                                                                                   | 2026-08-13 | Coverage Analysis produces the Coverage Result as part of the same capability; Coverage Result is not a separate processing stage. Business Requirement Coverage is a core FORGE capability and is not restricted to the MVP.                                                                      | Coverage Analysis has the information required to calculate and produce the resulting Business Requirement Coverage. Separating the result into an additional processing stage adds no functional value.                                                            |
| D-011                                                                                   | 2026-08-13 | Improvement of Requirement Coverage, including generation of additional Test Cases intended to improve coverage, is part of the MVP. The target coverage represents the minimum desired coverage and defaults to 95%. FORGE attempts to achieve at least the requested target.                     | The MVP should demonstrate not only the assessment of current Business Requirement Coverage but also the ability to propose Test Cases that improve insufficient coverage.                                                                                          |
| Generated Test Cases are proposals and do not constitute evidence of executed coverage. |            |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| D-012                                                                                   | 2026-08-15 | FORGE domain capabilities are independent of the interface through which FORGE is used.                                                                                                                                                                                                            | FORGE must be usable through different interfaces and may eventually be packaged as a reusable capability or Skill for other agents.                                                                                                                                |
| D-013                                                                                   | 2026-08-15 | The Workflow Orchestrator owns execution state, workflow transitions, iteration control, user-interaction state, and decisions about the next processing step. Domain capabilities produce domain results and do not control workflow progression.                                                 | Separating workflow coordination from domain analysis keeps capabilities independently usable and prevents them from becoming coupled to a specific execution flow or interface.                                                                                    |
| D-014                                                                                   | 2026-08-15 | FORGE domain capabilities do not remain active while waiting for user input. User interaction and execution resumption are coordinated by the Workflow Orchestrator and Interface.                                                                                                                 | This allows executions to be paused and resumed without coupling domain capabilities to a user session or runtime lifetime.                                                                                                                                         |
| D-015                                                                                   | 2026-08-15 | Domain capabilities do not depend directly on concrete external evidence systems, APIs, or SDKs. External systems are accessed through source abstractions and adapters.                                                                                                                           | New evidence sources must be introducible without modifying FORGE domain capabilities.                                                                                                                                                                              |
| D-016                                                                                   | 2026-08-15 | Domain capabilities that require LLM functionality depend on an LLM abstraction rather than on a specific provider or model.                                                                                                                                                                       | LLM providers and models must be replaceable without changing domain capability responsibilities.                                                                                                                                                                   |
| D-017                                                                                   | 2026-08-15 | FORGE domain results are structured domain concepts independent of their presentation. The Interface is responsible for deciding how those results are presented.                                                                                                                                  | The same domain results must be consumable through different interfaces or agents without introducing presentation concerns into the domain.                                                                                                                        |
| D-018                                                                                   | 2026-08-15 | Credentials, provider configuration, persistence mechanisms, concrete external integrations, and deployment mechanisms are infrastructure concerns and do not belong to FORGE domain capabilities.                                                                                                 | Infrastructure must remain replaceable without changing domain responsibilities.                                                                                                                                                                                    |
