# Product Decision Log
| ID    | Date       | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Reason                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ----- | ---------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| D-001 | 2026-08-07 | Primary user changed from QA Analyst to Product Manager.                                                                                                                                                                                         | The Product Manager has the broadest visibility of release risk while maintaining the same underlying capability. |
| D-002 | 2026-08-07 | The primary deliverable of the MVP is the Coverage Report.                                                                                                                                                                                       | It represents the highest-value outcome for the target user. |
| D-003 | 2026-08-07 | Requirements Discovery is an internal capability, not the product itself.                                                                                                                                                                        | The product exists to calculate Business Requirement Coverage. |
| D-004 | 2026-08-08 | FORGE is designed to analyse heterogeneous and incomplete Evidence Sources, including Jira Test Cases, Business Requirement documentation, User Stories, Bugs, Use Cases, Technical documentation, and other relevant evidence.                  | The capability must support different combinations of available evidence because Business Requirements and testing information may be dispersed, incomplete, or inconsistently documented. The MVP scope is intentionally narrower and is defined separately. |
| D-005 | 2026-08-07 | FORGE supports collaboration between roles but does not modify existing organizational responsibilities.                                                                                                                                         | Collaboration among different roles provides high value and a smooth process. |
| D-006 | 2026-08-08 | Glossary definitions are added progressively when discussions or identified ambiguities require them.                                                                                                                                            | Avoid defining universally understood terms prematurely and keep the glossary focused on terms that require explicit clarification. |
| D-007 | 2026-08-09 | The MVP calculates Business Requirement Coverage against existing Test Cases; Specifications are intermediate artefacts and Specification Validation is not a mandatory MVP gate.                                                                | The primary MVP outcome is Business Requirement Coverage and the functional risk derived from existing testing evidence. Requiring specification generation and approval before coverage would make an intermediate artefact a mandatory product outcome. |
| D-008 | 2026-08-09 | The MVP starts with existing Jira/XRay Test Cases as its primary Evidence Source; Jira Bugs and optional CSV notes may supplement the available testing evidence. Test Cases may contain steps or not. Other Evidence Source combinations are future extensions. | This provides a focused and demonstrable MVP while allowing additional evidence that may materially improve Requirements Discovery and coverage analysis, without expanding the MVP to the full heterogeneous Evidence Source capability. |
| D-009 | 2026-08-13 | The MVP Evidence Sources are expanded to include Confluence pages, Jira Test Cases, Jira Bugs, Jira User Stories, and Jira Epics. Business Requirements may be identified from explicit or implicit evidence across these sources. | Business Requirements may be distributed across project artefacts and may appear in Business Requirements documentation, Technical Requirements, Acceptance Criteria, User Story descriptions, Bug Expected Results, Epics, Test Cases, or other relevant evidence. The MVP must demonstrate this heterogeneous evidence analysis. |
| D-010 | 2026-08-13 | Coverage Analysis produces the Coverage Result as part of the same capability; Coverage Result is not a separate processing stage. Business Requirement Coverage is a core FORGE capability and is not restricted to the MVP. | Coverage Analysis has the information required to calculate and report the resulting Business Requirement Coverage. Separating the result into an additional processing stage adds no functional value. |
| D-011 | 2026-08-13 | Improvement of Requirement Coverage, including generation of additional Test Cases intended to improve coverage, is part of the MVP. The target coverage represents the minimum desired coverage and defaults to 95%. FORGE attempts to achieve at least the requested target. | The MVP should demonstrate not only the assessment of current Business Requirement Coverage but also the ability to propose Test Cases that improve insufficient coverage. Generated Test Cases are proposals and do not constitute evidence of executed coverage. |
