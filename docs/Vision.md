# Qué es FORGE y qué problema resuelve

## Purpose
FORGE exists to determine and justify the coverage of Business Requirements against existing Test Cases, even when the available information is incomplete, dispersed across multiple sources, and inconsistently documented.

Its objective is not to replace Business Analysts or Quality Assurance professionals, but to provide an AI-assisted capability that consolidates evidence, supports Requirements Discovery, and enables reliable coverage analysis.

## Product vision
FORGE enables organizations to understand which business capabilities are actually covered by their validation assets and where functional risk remains before software is released.
